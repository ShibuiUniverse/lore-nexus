**Desert of Setra**

Few have ventured into the Desert of Setra and returned to tell the
tale. Desolate and unending, no one knows where it ends or what is on
the other side. Long ago, a sage group of prophets made their home in
the desert - but no one has heard from them in a generation.

**Women Warriors**

With the legendary warriors cursed to the Soulless Citadel and the Dark
Ones in relentless pursuit, the Light Walkers needed a new guard, and
they needed it fast. Day in and day out, training and honing their
skills, they became the fiercest warriors the land had ever seen --- and
the Legend of the Light Walker Women Warriors was born.

**Dragon Islands**

The Dragons' tale is as old as the Light Walkers - and they too have
chosen their own paths and allegiances. Known for their mystical forge,
the dragons are the smiths of all weapons of power in Baransu. The Sea
Dragons make their home in the southern isles where volcanoes carve out
lava tubes that power their forges.

**Light Walker Old Village**

The Old Village is the ancestral home of the Light Walkers. It is the
home where Sensei Murkai and the first people laid down their packs
after traveling the Realm to survey the Almighty's creation. It is from
the Old Village that the Light Walkers sent out their warriors and
watched over Baransu. Once a beacon of hope for all the realm, the Old
Village is now desolate and abandoned after the curse.

**Bay of Monsters**

All seafaring folk avoid the Bay if they can help it - gigantic water
snakes, ship-sized squid, and a beast so haunting and deadly it's only
spoken of as "the Shadow". It is said, when the Shadow comes over a ship
and blots out the sun, it's already too late - the ship will never be
seen again. The Pirates of Fukushu have been known to brave the Bay when
a prize seems worth the risk, but the gamble is great and the voyage has
been known to drive even the fiercest pirate mad.

**Forbidden Forest**

The Forbidden Forest is a magical forest that acts as a single organism.
The trees speak to one another and shift their formations in response to
any humans that try to enter. The animals have their own common language
and are wary of humans. Beautiful vegetation can become deadly if the
forest feels threatened. Legend says that the first seed of the garden
was planted by the Almighty himself from the Upper Realm and from it
sprung the first Baransu Elm - from which every other elm in Baransu was
seeded.

**Canyon of Despair**

Beautiful and lush, the Canyon of Despair looks more like a valley of
delight - but nestled in the heart of the Forbidden Forest, this Canyon
can be deceptive in its welcome. A seeming haven from the dangers of the
Forest, one soon discovers that the Canyon is the nesting ground of
dragons who don't take kindly to visitors.

**Lake Shamakai**

The oldest and purest source of freshwater on Baransu, Lake Shamakai is
the domain of the Undying Lacus People. Waters from the Lake flow
through the Valley and irrigate the Forbidden Forest. It is said that
the Lacus People guard a secret at the bottom of the Lake - but [none
other than the Lacus dare to brave the depths of the Lake as the
Shamakai Eel Dragon hunts the waters for Baransu-born.]{.mark}

**Lacus People**

One of the original three groups of Undying Ones the Almighty created in
the Upper Realm, the Lacus People proved loyal and honorable when others
betrayed him. The Almighty sent them as his emissaries to live at Lake
Shamakai when he created the Baransu Realm - transforming the beautiful
Lacus to people of the sea and land. He entrusted them as guardians of
the portal to the Shadow Realm - but no Baransu-born knows where the
portal lies.

**Kubu Forest\
\
Magical Bioluminescent, glowing Bamboo Forrest\
**

On the southern border of the Upper Mountains is a quiet and serene
forest of bamboo trees. The forest is home to an elusive tribe, The Kubu
People, who planted the grove generations ago. The people loved and
cultivated the forest with their own peculiar magic - and the fate of
both are intertwined.

**Montes Giants**

One of the original three groups of Undying Ones created in the Upper
Realm, the Montes People were given the Almighty's gift of wisdom. When
Khaonai led the Robur in a rebellion against the Almighty, the Montes
were faithful to their creator and fought alongside the Lacus to
overcome the rebels. But the Montes began to become prideful of their
wisdom - their egos swelling. Because of this, the Almighty sent the
Montes to the Baransu Realm where they began to transform into haughty,
formidable rock formations and slowly became part of the mountains
themselves[. Feared in ages past, the wise Montes Rock Giants have
become a distant legend to the people of Baransu. Only a few in great
need of wisdom have trekked the Upper Mountains to seek their counsel
with trembling.]{.mark}

**Fukushu Bay and Town**

Home to outcasts, runaways and pirates of all kinds - Fukushu Bay Town
is a wild place with a law unto itself. The Bay is wide with temperate
waters, but jagged rocks on all but one side have taken more than a few
ships in a storm. The docks, however, are a welcome relief to fisherman
and pirate alike.

**Pirates of Fukushu**

The Pirates of Fukushu rule the waters around the Bay and frequent the
Town where no law challenges them other than their own code. Cast out
and runaway Baransu-born sailors crew ships hunting for treasure - each
band scheming how to keep the others from the prize.

A fleet of dark pirates - a mix of living and unnatural creatures - are
also said to haunt the waters. The Baransu-born look for treasure while
the dark pirates are compelled by foul masters seeking only the darkest
powers of the Baransu realm and beyond.

**Reckoning Island**

Far to the nor' east and past treacherous cold waters that turn icy
without warning, is Reckoning Island. The waters are only passable in
the warmest part of the year but it is then that the they teem with
ravenous sea monsters who've made the journey for the meaty pickings of
the thaw. Some in the realm believe there is some magic at play on the
island as lights in the heavens shower down colorful streaks over it at
the darkest time of the year.

**Portal to Realms**

Unpredictable and dangerous - the lure of the Portal to Realms has
enticed a number of Baransu-born to seek adventure - or escape - in a
different realm. And, though no one has seen it happen, many suspect
that others from different realms have entered Baransu by the Portal. It
is said the Portal moves periodically, but no one understands why or
how - or indeed, if anyone can track its movements. The Dark Pirates
have long sought a ruby rumored to control the Portal, and will stop at
nothing to claim it.

**Soulless Citadel**

Towering atop a floating island trapped between realms, the Soulless
Citadel is a fortress that holds no one in but lets no one escape. Its
cold, dark fortifications give no comfort and echo every doubt and
desperation in a warrior's heart. Sensei Lux and the Light Walker
Warriors were cursed to the Citadel by Tenebris in the Great Battle for
Zillarnia. As Tenebris saw his imminent defeat, he used the last of his
dark magic to banish the warriors and cursed each Light Walker male upon
his coming of age to join the original warriors in nightmarish limbo.

**Valley of Zillarnia**

[Nestled between two mountain ranges is the fertile Valley of Zillarnia.
Steeped in heroic tales of old, the Valley was a place of peace, harmony
and abundance for generations. Children roamed freely between its
villages and diverse cultures flourished - weaving a vibrant tapestry of
arts, weaponry, and innovations. At the heart of the Valley was the
Light Walker Village - a refuge for Zillarnians and travelers in need.
It was the home of the fearless Light Walker Clan whose unmatched

vanished in a veil of mystery. There are whispers that a remnant has
survived, but none can be found. Legend says that when the days turn
their darkest, the Light Walkers will return and reestablish the peace
and plenty of the days of old - and this time, for the whole
