**SHIBUI UNIVERSE ORIGIN STORY**

> The Almighty is. The Almighty has always been. The Almighty will
> forever be.
>
> The Almighty has made all that is - the heavens and the depths, the
> elements, the creatures, and the beings - and all are his.
>
> Great and mysterious are his ways.
>
> In the days long before our people were given life, he made the
> Undying Ones in the Upper Realm - each to bear a gift of his nature -
>
> The Montes- to bear his wisdom and knowledge
>
> The Lacus- to bear his loyalty and honor

And

> The Robur- to bear his strength
>
> To each of these, the Almighty gave a precious gem - instilled with
> the essence of the gift. There was harmony and peace, plenty and
> rest... and none feared the bitter taste of death.
>
> But ambition grew in the heart of Khaonai, the greatest of the Robur.

He loved the power of his strength so much that he began to seek power
beyond what the Almighty had given him. Convinced his creator was
withholding from him, Khaonai began to despise the Almighty.

Khaonai's bitterness and hate birthed a new magic - the Dark Magic. And
he conjured a weapon to wield it - the Bident of Khaonai.

> Khaonai soon challenged the Almighty to a great battle, leading the
> Robur people in his rebellion. The Montes and the Lacus, faithful to
> the Almighty, fought alongside their creator. The heavens shook and
> the depths were torn asunder. The animals scattered, now terrified of
> their masters. The seas roiled, the land splintered.
>
> In the end, the Almighty and the faithful overcame Khaonai and the
> Robur - but like a disease, the Dark Magic had already spread.
>
> Knowing that he could not extinguish his Undying Ones, the Almighty
> created the Shadow Realm. There he banished Khaonai and the Robur -
> his betrayers would never stand in the glory of the Upper Realm again.

And though the Montes and the Lacus had been faithful, the Almighty knew
he must act. Even now the Montes were becoming prideful of their wisdom.
And so he created another realm - the

**2**

> Baransu Realm. There he sent the Montes - transforming them into
> giants to fit the size of their pride.

And because the Lacus stayed true to their honorable, loyal nature, the
Almighty made them guardians of the door between the Shadow Realm and
the Baransu Realm. From their new dwelling at the bottom of Lake
Shamakai in Baransu, the Lacus would make certain those banished to the
Shadow Realm could never breach the door.

> Grieved by the Undying Ones, the Almighty wanted to begin again. He
> created and placed a new people in the Baransu Realm - the Novus
> People. No divine wisdom or might - not even loyalty inborn. No - they
> would have their own will - to choose as they wished, and to seek
> their own paths.
>
> Their choices would bring life eternal - or never-ending death.
>
> \* \* \*
>
> The Almighty wished to walk closely with his new creation so he
> descended to Baransu in human form - concealing his identity. He
> wanted to teach the Novus how to wield his power and to watch over his
> dominion.
>
> For years the Almighty trained and prepared Sensei Murkai- a man of
> pure heart, and his brother Turkan- driven and strong-willed. At last,
> the Almighty took them to the Upper Realm to show them the origin of
> their creation - and to reveal his true nature and power.
>
> But even as the Almighty took the brothers further and further into
> his confidence, Turkan began to hear a faint Whisper - a whisper that
> there was more - that the Almighty would withhold power and glory from
> him. The Whisper grew louder and more insistent until one day Turkan
> tried to find its source.

It led him to a place he'd never seen before - a dark fortress. It
seemed to be waiting for him. As he approached, the Whisper beckoned
him - assuring him that his true destiny was within. At last he found
its source in a dark, hidden chamber - the very Bident of Khaonai.
Knowing of its power from legend, Turkan immediately took it up and the
intoxication of the Dark Magic entered his heart.

> Turkan used the bident to transport himself back to the [Baransu
> Realm]{.mark}. And now, transformed by the Dark Magic and drunk with
> his new power, he brought a wave of terror and suffering everywhere
> his shadow darkened. For centuries, he laid waste to the land and
> people, turning his victims into mindless beasts of his bidding. Our
> people now called him "Tenebris" which means "darkness comes".

But after Tenebris took up the Bident of Khaonai, the Almighty forged a
weapon that could hold him at bay and would one day bring restoration
and peace to the Realm - the Staff of the Almighty. He chose Sensei
Murkai and his Light Walker clan to be the keepers of the Staff so long
as they would walk with him and follow his way.
