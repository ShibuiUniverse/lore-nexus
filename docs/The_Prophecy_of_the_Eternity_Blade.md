# The Prophecy of the Eternity Blade

**Background of the Blade's Creation:**

Kurogami, once a wise and noble Dragon who spent his days protecting
those of the Baransu Realm, was the best blacksmith known ever to walk
the realm. Known for fastening great relics of the past. But, he fell
into the deceit of the Dark Whisper. The Dark whisper slowly hardened
Kurogami's heart with a lust for more. Kurogami knew of the gemstones
the almighty gifted to the undying ones in the upper realm. He knew of
their vast power. And for what? For them to never be used after
Khaonai's rebellion.

Kurogami grew tireless of being the noble protector -- he wanted more
power. The Whisper convinced him that Khoanai could bestow that power
upon him if he could find a way to free him. Kurogami forged a blade, a
blade to wield all the gemstones of the Almighty. The Emerald of Wisdom,
Pearl of Honor, Baransu Ruby, Opal of Strength, and the Diamon of the
Almighty Staff -- these five gemstones combined to wield power unseen by
anyone but the Almighty himself.\
\
Engulfed by the lust for power, Kurogami forged the Blade in the depths
of the Fukushū Sea at the underwater volcano Erdekan. Before Kurogami
and the dark ones started their quest to gather all the gemstones and
wield the great Blade of Eternity, the brave Ryujinshi, Kurogami's own
sister, stood in their way. A great battle unraveled. Kurogami and
Ryujinshi, brother and sister, fought until the bloody end. Ryujinshi
defeated Kurogami and seized the great weapon of destruction.
Heartbroken by the loss of her brother, she disappeared into the depths
of the underground lava tubes running throughout the easter coast of
Baransu and made a vow to protect the Blade until her last breath and
never to let it fall into the wrong hands again.\
\
**The Double Prophecy:\
\
**One of great power and burning with rage, A direct descendent of
Turkan (Tenebris), shall wield the Blade of Eternity and unlock the
doors to the Shadow Realm - All hell will break loose on all the Realms.
Darkness will rise, and Khaonai shall seek his revenge on the Almighty
once again.

Crafted in pure darkness by greed and power - redeemed to light by one
worthy. Chosen by birth, named by wisdom - A story of light and
darkness, two sides to every coin - A direct descendent of Sensei
Murakai, born to royal blood and of a pure heart shall win the favor of
the undying ones, gather the gemstones, wield the blade made for
darkness and turn it to light! Restoring the balance and freeing all
people from the whisper's grasp.\
