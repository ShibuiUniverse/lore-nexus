
*Thump. Thump. Thump.*

"Lux... I don't know how long the door will hold them off." Vi, Lux's
brother, yells while pushing all his weight to support the doors.

Lux sits quietly and calmly in the center of the cold, moist citadel
floor. His legs crossed, and eyes closed. It's not the first time He's
felt like the darkness has won... but he knows those are just lies.

A dark mist, like thick fog on a winter's morning, rises around Lux. It
swirls, and in its misty darkness, crimson embers flicker. Like a ball
of energy constantly moving and shifting. With every move, you can hear
the scream of agony, the hiss of the darkness slipping from its grasp.

With each pass of Lux's ears, it whispers.

"You've failed, Lux." echoes the whisper. "Look at you. You sit and
pray, while your men are consumed. They want me, they want the
darkness."

Lux remains calm. His breathing exact. His eyes still closed.

"The Almighty has turned his cheek from you. Turn to me... I can free
you."

Thump. Thump. Thump. The doors pound and bend with pressure. The sound
of fingernails scratching on the other side. Groaning and shrieks of
pain. Vi presses with all his strength against the bending doors. He
rests his forehead on on the wood. *Brothers, what have you become?* He
thinks. "Lux, I need you! Brother, it's time!"\
\
The whisper swirls more violently around Lux. Bursts of dark red and
purple embers erupt in its swirling mist. "You should have heard your
daughter scream\... Do you know what she said?"

A bead of sweat slides down Lux's forehead, but still his composure
remains steady. "She screamed for your name... She begged me to stop...
But Daddy wasn't there." The Whisper laughs a quiet, evil laugh. Lux
gives no attention to his words. The Whisper visually erupts. *How can
this man, this weak man, still not give in!\
\
*"Where is HE!? Where is the Almighty now!? HE'S TURNED HIS EYES OFF
YOU. You are LOST!" The Whisper shrieks as its voice cracks with anger.

The pounding on the door and the groaning grow louder. More Violent. The
Rocks on the floor around Lux shake with the Thumping of the Door.

"BROTHER! It's Time!" Vi yells as he strains against the doors.\
Lux's eyes blast open, glowing bright white. His essence seems to
pulsate with power. As if time and light warp around his energy. THE
ALMIGHTY POWER.

"AH HAAA HA!!!! YES!" Vi screams from the doorway. "The Almighty Power
is back, Baby!!!"

Lux looks to the whisper with his glowing eyes. "AWAY FROM ME! Your
words are lies for the weak!" The Whisper lets out a painful shriek as
it disappears into thin air. Lux looks to his brother, Vi. "Save who we
can and push the lost down to the dungeon." Vi nods his head, sweat
pouring down his forehead at this point.

"Open the doors, and let them come!" Lux yells as he gathers his
Almighty Power. He turns his arms in a circle, then quickly makes a
formation, and the Almighty symbol glows in the center.\
\
The doors blast open violently. Wood Splinters flying in all directions.
Vi withdraws his Black Dragon Daggers. He flexes his arms, and the
Almighty Power surges through his veins and into the blades. They burst
into a blueish-yellow light. His eyes illuminated just as his Lux's.

"Ohhhhhh, We are soooo Back!!!" Vi Yells. Lux can't help but smirk.

Through the doors, the Dark Horde of their fallen deformed brothers
cascade at them like rabbit beasts.

"Almighty, cover us. Make a way to save as many as we can," Lux whispers
to himself.
