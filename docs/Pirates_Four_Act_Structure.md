# FOUR ACT STRUCTURE — PIRATES OF FUKUSHU

*(Still rough/incomplete — key beats established, later acts need further development)*

---

## ACT ONE — ANTONIA

**Setup:** Young Antonia of Zillarnia marries Vegas even though her family is unsure of him. We see a tattoo of the Almighty's symbol on her wrist. Next, Antonia is pregnant and she and Vegas rejoice — Vegas "gives" the unborn baby something... something that Antonia will keep with her that we will recognize — a stone or cloth or something...

**Inciting Incident:** Vegas runs away with the ruby.

**Rising Action:** Antonia's sister shoots and kills Vegas and Antonia (pregnant) sails away on a boat with the ruby.

**Act One Climax:** Antonia is at the brink of dehydration/starvation stuck at sea — she prays to the Almighty — the symbol on her wrist glows — and he has mercy on her and sends a sea monster to push her to land where she nearly immediately goes into labor on the Island of Tortuga (or whatever we call it).

---

## ACT TWO — DAVIE

**Setup:** Teenaged Davie is on his father's ship and wants to see action but his father, Captain Vane, forbids it. We see Vane holding counsel with a vaporous black smoke (the Dark Whisper) and we learn that Captain Vane is a notorious pirate from Earth who was banished to this realm many years ago. It seems Vane has made a deal with the Dark Whisper — to give the soul of his own "seed" when the Whisper shows him the way to the portal back to Earth.

**Inciting Incident:** Davie stows away on a landing boat with pirates sent by Vane to find the magical sextant that will show the location of the portal back to Earth. The pirates all fall to the islands' traps except for Davie, who plans to find the sextant and bring it back to his father to make him proud.

**Rising Action:** Davie stumbles down a ravine on the island, bumps his head, and loses consciousness. When he wakes up, he is in a net/trap hoisted high above a lagoon with half man/half fish people swimming below (the Lacus). (OR when he wakes, he is being nursed by the Lacus people.) The first mate returns to Vane's ship and lies, saying that Davie has died along with the rest of the party (the First Mate cares about Davie). Vane is enraged because he needed Davie for his deal with the Dark Whisper. Now Vane's soul is in hock to the Dark Whisper.

## ACT THREE — 7 YEARS LATER

*The action cuts back and forth between Davie and Antonia more often now until their stories come together.*

**Rising Action (Antonia)** — Antonia and her son have a metal-working shop/smithy. A customer tries to make unwelcome advances and Antonia's young son warns the customer off. Antonia chastises her son saying he is just a boy and he could get hurt acting like that. Her son says he does not want to be weak and wants to protect her. He asks about the tattoo on her wrist — Antonia explains that the Almighty saved them from the sea and delivered them to this place. This is his sign. Back in their sleeping area, Antonia feels around in her apron for the ruby. It's not there and there is a hole in the pocket. Panicked, she looks and thankfully finds it a few feet away on the floor. She stuffs it down in the bottom of a trunk/chest.

**Rising Action (Davie)** — Davie has been fully embraced by the inhabitants of the Island (the Lacus people) and is now like them in every way except he does not have a fish tail. He seems to have no memory of how he got there (amnesia from his fall). He and a Lacus girl clearly have feelings for each other. The elders (or maybe the "Prince of the Lacus"?) discuss whether they should tell Davie about his past and his father.

[NOTE: Open question on Davie's love interest — should she be a Lacus girl or the daughter of a human tribe king who co-lives on the island? If she is Lacus, the question arises of whether the baby would be undying and whether she eventually has to choose between being Lacus or human. Both options have interesting story potential. Keep open for now.]

**Rising Action (Antonia)** — The villagers are starting to murmur — the same sea monster sighted the day Antonia arrived those years ago is now trolling the island again and the fish are frightened away. The villagers are sure she has brought a curse on them. Antonia and her son's business is starting to suffer. Antonia meets an older, blind man on the road whom she brings food to. Her son is getting into trouble at the village school.

**Rising Action (Davie)** — The Lacus elders (or the Prince) show Davie Cornelius' journal, which tells about the infamy of Davie's father. Davie's memory is triggered when they give him back his own pirate gear — but he did not know his father was the terror described in the journal. Captain Vane had convinced Davie that he'd been the one wronged by the Navy. (This is when we discover the rest of the backstory: about Julius and his crew, how the portal works, that you need the ruby and that the sea monster guards the portal, etc.) Davie decides to disown his father: "this is my family now" (about the Lacus) and marries the Lacus girl.

**Rising Action (Antonia)** — Antonia's son comes into the smithy wearing a headband from Antonia's trunk of things from her family that she has packed away, wanting to forget. Antonia scolds him for getting into her things and asks what else he found/took (she is afraid he's found the ruby). Her son demands to know about his family and his father but she refuses to tell him. The old blind man (now called "Uncle" — clearly an ongoing relationship) gives Antonia and her son meaningful advice, saying he can "see" what others can't. When she gets home, Antonia takes the ruby out of the chest and puts it at the bottom of a vase.

**Rising Action (Davie / Captain Vane)** — Time pushes forward and Davie's new wife is pregnant. One of the Lacus elders (or the Prince) tells Davie that he and his wife must flee. He can see it in the sky — the red moon is coming, the atmosphere is unsettled and the portal's location is about to shift after years of quiet, so Vane is bound to come looking for Davie and the sextant. (The coming shift has aggravated the Dark Whisper, who visits Vane and tells him his son is still alive — that his First Mate lied to him all those years ago. Vane kills the first mate for this.) The Lacus elder/Prince says he has seen the one who holds the ruby in a vision: "She is also being prepared for what is coming, though she fights the mantle." Davie and his wife prepare to leave — but she goes into labor just as Vane's ship arrives. Davie's wife dies in labor, but a healthy baby girl is born. Davie is fighting the pirates off but one grabs the newborn while Davie is pinned down. It's a standoff for the sextant, which Davie surrenders because they promise to hand the baby over for it. But they take the sextant and the baby girl, saying all they need is "Captain Vane's seed" — so either will do.

[NOTE: Need to add a beat where Davie discovers when/where the Lacus or the Almighty appears to those who cry out — perhaps at a full moon — so he sets out in hope to find them. At one point they do appear, maybe after he cries out to the Almighty when he gives up hope.]

**Rising Action (Davie)** — Desperate to get his daughter back, Davie sets off on his small fishing boat but is dashed in the waves outside the island's bay. The Dark Whisper comes to him, urging murderous thoughts about his father. (Maybe the Dark Whisper reveals that Vane plans to sacrifice Davie — give his soul to the Dark Whisper so that Vane can get back to Earth for revenge.) Davie is about to get smashed by a huge wave, but at the last moment he is delivered onto Julius' half-enchanted ship by the sea monster. After pleading with and surrendering to the Almighty, Davie convinces Julius and the crew — who are half-cursed because they mutinied when Julius sailed them through the portal to keep Vane from returning — that he is not like his father, and that he will help them get back to Earth and be free of their curse if they will help him get his daughter back.

[NOTE: At first Julius' crew doesn't listen and tries to kill Davie — then something like a divine protection stops them, they all shout "Captain Vane!" and then Davie reveals he is the son. He tells them everything, promises to help defeat his father, and they finally agree.]

**Rising Action (Davie / Captain Vane)** — Now with the sextant and his newborn granddaughter (whom he plans to give to the Dark Whisper in exchange for his own soul), Vane is further enraged to learn from the Dark Whisper that there is yet another element needed to access the portal — the Baransu ruby, which has power over the sea monster that guards the portal. The Dark Whisper tells Vane the ruby will flash when the portal changes position, so they all watch the horizon. Meanwhile on Julius'/Davie's ship, they are also watching the horizon. Davie watches curiously as the sea monster swims around their ship. Julius orders his men to harpoon it but Davie stops them: "this beast brought me to you — it has a part in this, though I don't know what."

[NOTE: Need a female character who takes care of the baby on board — maybe a rough female pirate who was Davie's childhood friend on the ship and also thought he was dead. She takes the baby and makes sure it is okay.]

**Rising Action (Antonia)** — "Uncle" is having dinner in Antonia and her son's home. He has clearly become part of the family. Uncle says to Antonia: "For what's coming — you will need to take up your family's mantle — do not refuse it, reclaim it." The old man should know nothing about her family and their charge, so she is baffled — but she is distracted because the ruby begins to glow from inside the vase where she'd hidden it. The power is so strong that the vase breaks. Antonia's son is in awe: "What is this? Why have you not told me about this? What is it? What does it do?" The old man is no longer there. Antonia tries to calm her son, but has a sense that danger is coming — sure enough, on the horizon is a large ship coming into port and warning bells start to ring in the village.

### MIDPOINT OF ACT THREE

Coming for the ruby, Captain Vane's crew (half cursed human, half Forgotten Ones) attack the island where Antonia and her son are. Antonia has wrapped the ruby and clutches it as they run, but the red light shines through. Her son tells her to "use" the ruby to save them, but she says she doesn't know how. They try to flee but the attackers gain on them. She hands the ruby to her son and tells him to bury it quickly and then hide in a tree while she prepares to fight (she has been trained). Her son is amazed to see his mother fight. She fights well, but there are too many — she is in a chokehold with a Forgotten One threatening to kill her and her son (whom they found) if she does not hand over the ruby. Things start to go dark as she loses oxygen. The next thing she sees is a human pirate standing over her — this is Davie, with Julius' crew.

**Rising Action / Obstacle (Antonia/Davie)** — When she fully comes to, Antonia realizes she and her son have been brought aboard Julius' ship by Davie and the men. Her son indicates that he has the ruby safely on him. "Her" sea monster trails the ship. She is panicked at being "captured," but Davie assures her they mean no harm and explains about his father, his daughter, and why Vane was coming for the ruby — to get back to Earth. Davie explains that Vane will never stop chasing her unless they meet him head-on and contain him. Davie needs Antonia and the ruby to lure Vane into a "parley" so he can get his daughter back and make sure his father never hurts anyone again. Antonia doesn't want any part of it. Her son has heard everything and pleads: "Mom — we have to help them — you have the power!" She insists that the ruby only makes people hurt one another and brings nothing but pain — she will not. She makes her son hand her the ruby, which she stows in her clothing/wrap.

*(From here forward, only high-level plot points — less fully developed so far.)*

**Rising Action / Obstacle (Antonia & Davie)** — (Maybe?) Antonia has a dream that night aboard the boat — she sees Vegas being shot and then her sister saying, "You've brought this on yourself — on all of us!" She wakes feeling for the ruby and can't find it. In her panic, the Dark Whisper swirls in and taunts her that Davie has taken it and taken her son too (her son is not in his hammock): "You should know better — men's hunger for power will always overtake them — you cannot trust..." She runs up to the ship's deck and sees her son trying to give the ruby to Davie because he's frustrated that his Mom won't use it. But Davie refuses it, saying that it is his mother's and he would be no better than his father, Captain Vane, if he took it. Antonia overhears this.

[NOTE: Consider — if Davie's wife just died and they have his baby, he might be a lot more irrational here, not patient. Also: maybe only a Light Walker can use the ruby — so Davie couldn't take it even if he wanted to. Either way, this is the moment where Antonia begins to see she can trust Davie.]

**Rising Action / Obstacle (Captain Vane)** — The Dark Whisper tells Vane that Davie and the Zillarnian woman are together and planning to trick him into giving Davie's daughter back. Vane has a plan to outwit them and sends an invitation with one of his crew to our heroes for parley.

**Rising Action / Obstacle (Antonia & Davie)** — (Maybe?) Still frustrated by his mother's refusal to act, Antonia's son steals away in one of the rowboats with the ruby to the Island of Reckoning for the parley, forcing their hands to go after him. Antonia is devastated and quickly urges Davie to follow.

**Rising Action / Obstacle** — Still to be fully developed. She should cry out to the Almighty again. She will put on her family's headband as a sign that she's accepting her role.

[NOTE: Use the sea monster or bring "Uncle" in again here. Uncle is either the Almighty in his "old sensei" form or an angel of the Almighty. Perhaps he appears after she makes her choice — as a random fisherman on the Island of Reckoning (which is uninhabited), or one of the crewmembers she hadn't noticed until now.]

### CLIMAX / MOMENT OF APPARENT DEFEAT

Vane captures Antonia's son with the ruby on his approach to the island — he was lying in wait behind the bay's cliffs to ambush our heroes arriving for the parley. Much to Vane's fury, the ruby won't "work" for him. But now Vane has additional leverage over both Antonia and Davie.

*Still need to work out the full chess moves to resolution — but the showdown area is inside a sea cave with both land and water (the "Cave of Sorrows"?). Key moments needed:*

- Antonia to fight and kick ass
- Antonia's son to do something heroic — trusting his mom, willing to sacrifice himself, or showing real growth
- The Sea Monster to play a key part — we finally see how it guards the portal
- Davie to be tempted to kill his father but choose not to. Ideally he sacrifices himself for his baby daughter without actually dying — something tied to the promise Vane made to the Dark Whisper
- Davie to stick to his word — taking Julius' charge of guarding the portal, and sending Julius and his loyal crew back to Earth along with Vane (who somehow regains his soul but will face justice on Earth — Captain Julius will see to it). Maybe the crew that mutinied is given a choice: go back to Earth and face court martial, or stay and serve Captain Antonia faithfully on the ship.
