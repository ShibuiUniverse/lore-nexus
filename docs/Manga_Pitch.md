**Eternal Blade\
\
Logline:**\
[Desperate to save her cursed father from the Soulless Citadel, young
warrior Emerald discovers she is the key to an ancient prophecy of light
and darkness. Her only hope lies in a fabled dark artifact---the Eternal
Blade. To wield it, she must embark on a quest to find the gemstones
created at the beginning of time. The fate of her father---and the
realm---lies in her hands.]{.mark}

**[Story Synopsis]{.mark}**:\
\
Set in the Baransu Realm, there was once peace, innovation, and harmony,
where one's children could go to a neighboring village without worry or
doubt. Protected by the Legendary Light Walker Clan, anointed by the
Almighty to wield His power, they stood firm and kept order with
humility and strength. Until one day, they faced an ancient darkness. A
day they knew would come for ages. A battle set in prophecy for hundreds
of years. The Dark Warlord, Tenebris, and his mindless hoard of
Forgotten Ones finally stood face to face with the Legendary Light
Walker Clan. After a fierce battle of blades, beasts, and magic, the
light walkers overcame them, but at a high cost. With Tenebris' last
words, he uttered a curse, sending the Light Walker Warriors to a
hellish Limbo in "The Soulless Citadel." Every warrior from that fateful
day and every firstborn son on his eighteenth birthday were to vanish to
the Citadel - leaving the women and children to fend for themselves with
the ever-present "Dark Whisper," seeking to corrupt the hearts of the
Light and rid the Light Walkers of the Realm.\
\
Left in charge of the Light Walker Clan, Melea, wife of the former
Sensei Lux, had to take action. She led the Clan into the Forbidden
Forrest to seek refuge and a place to rebuild. Melea guided them with
the Power of The Almighty and trained the remaining women into the
fiercest women warriors the Realm had seen. They trained day in and day
out, honing their skills to face the darkness once again and hoping one
day to set their men free from the Citadel. After ten years had passed,
the women were prepared, but had found no way to break the curse. Until,
one day, a stranger from the Desert of Setra appeared.

Emerald, the eldest daughter of Sensei Lux and Melea and future heir to
the clan, broke the clan rules, left the forest to save the stranger,
and brought him back into the Forbidden Forrest. The stranger, Kilian of
the Kenshi Clan in the Desert of Setra, brought with him an old scroll
from the Tenshi Prophets. The scroll he presented to the High Council
spoke of an old untold Prophecy---one of two sides. The prophecy speaks
of the Eternal Blade - a blade forged by the great Dragon Kurogami,
forged in darkness to free the Dark One, Khaonai, from his shackles in
the Shadow Realm. The prophecy was known that a Light Walker turned dark
would gather all the Gem Stones of the Realm and free Khaonai from his
shackles, bringing chaos and darkness over the land-- But Kilian's
scroll spoke of a second Prophecy yet unknown. The prophecy states that
one direct blood descendent of the first Sensei, Sensei Murakai, would
gather all the Almighty's gemstones and wield them as one with the
Eternal Blade, freeing the realm of the curse and darkness. The only
last-standing warrior of the direct bloodline of Sensei Murakai is
16-year-old Emerald, the adventurous and rebellious daughter of Sensei
Lux who's avoided responsibility since the day she lost her Farther.

After a surprise attack from the Dark Ones led by Kage, a former Light
Walker Clan turned Dark, the High Counsel decides that Emerald must
leave to collect all four gemstones and seek the Eternal Blade. With
Kage and the Dark Ones close on their tracks, Emerald and Kilian set off
to gather the gemstones. Their first stop leads them to the Upper
Mountains to seek the Montes Giants, where many have fallen in seeking
their divine wisdom.

Emerald and Kilian win favor in the Montes' eyes, and they grant her the
Gem Stone under one condition -- That Grummel, the wisest of the Montes
and Keeper of the Emerald of Wisdom, accompany them on their journey.
Emerald, Kilian, and their newfound friend Grummel descend the Upper
Mountains only to be ambushed by a surprise attack from the Dark Ones.
They cut through the Kubu forest. The Kubu People, protectors of the
Almighty's creation, help them narrowly escape Kage and the Dark Ones.
After escaping through the Kubu Forest, Emerald and her crew seek
answers in Fukushu Bay.

As fate would have it, the three get marooned on a small rowboat in the
middle of the Fukushu Sea and have a run-in with the Pirates of Fukushu,
Captain Antonia, and a ragtag band of Pirates. After boarding the ship,
Emerald discovers that Captain Antonia is the long-lost sister of her
mother, Melea. After a heartfelt family reunion, Emerald explains to
Antonia her quest and what she has set out to complete. Antonia, feeling
the pull of the Almighty's Power on Emerald, reveals that she holds the
Baransu Ruby and the Eternal Blade. Antonia gifts the Ruby and the Blade
to Emerald and blesses her on her quest to move forward. This only
leaves two more gemstones to collect: the Pearl of Honor held by the
Lacus people and the Almighty Diamond that is lost with the staff of the
Almighty.\
\
As all seems to be falling into place for Emerald and her crew to finish
their quest and fulfill the prophecy, disaster strikes. Kilian tries to
reveal the truth of a secret he keeps and his affection for Emerald, but
as he does, an item falls from his pocket: a dark one's tracker whistle.
The reason they had been tracked so easily by the dark ones and Kage was
because Kilian had been secretly leading them on. Emerald is crushed.
She had fallen for Kilian, but he had already betrayed them. Antonia and
the Pirates of Fukushu make Kilian walk the plank and leave him stranded
in the middle of the Fukushu Sea.

Emerald and the Pirates reach the shore near the Lacus people's dwelling
at sunrise. Antonia encourages Emerald on her journey, reminding her
that even though her heart is broken, she must continue the quest to
save her father and break the curse. Grummel and Emerald continue on
solo to reach Lake Shamakai to meet with the Lacus Queen. Queen Aurelia
meets the two at the shore of the lake. Emerald tells her of their
journey, the new prophecy, and the curse. Queen Aurelia already knows of
their journey and that she has been waiting for their arrival, although
she was unsure they would even make it. She gives Emerald her blessing
to take the Pearl of Honor, but mentions that it is only bestowed upon
those who can pass the test and win the favor of the Pearl's guardian at
the bottom of Lake Shamakai.

[**Character Descriptions:\
\
Emerald (Light Walker Clan) -** Daughter of the infamous Sensei Lux,
Emerald has a lot on her shoulders. Growing up without her father from
age six until now has worn on her self-confidence. She is adventurous at
heart with a hint of rebelliousness. She is always caught by her mother
or grandmother sneaking out of the Light Walker Camp to explore the
mysterious Forbidden Forrest. Just 16 years old, she is thrown into a
pivotal decision -- to leave the forest to save a mysterious voice
(Kilian) yelling for help, which is greatly forbidden, or let the savage
Equinoi tear him apart. Her decision to save Kilian embarks her on a new
destiny she reluctantly takes, to gather the Almighty Gemstones, the Key
to Eternal Blade, and try to break the curse of the Soulless
Citadel.]{.mark}

**Kilian (Tenshi Clan) -** Coming from the Prophets in the Desert of
Setra in the west and the son of the leader of the Tenshi Clan, Kilian
is not a warrior but has a brave and endearing heart. His quirky sense
of humor and big mouth often gets him into trouble. He is torn by a
secret he keeps, but at the same time, he is drawn to Emerald's
adventurous spirit and drive to follow the Almighty's ways.\
\
**Kage (Light Walker Clan turned Dark One) -** 21 years old, Kage was
once a loyal and strong follower of the Almighty's ways and an integral
part of the Light Walker Clan. After a horrible turn of events, Kage's
heart grew bitter and cold. Kage blamed the Almighty for all her losses
and turned from Him and her people. The Dark Whisper beckoned her to
follow it, promising power and strength. Although she has been missing
for years now\...

**Grummel (Montes) -** One of the undying Montes Giants. Transformed
into rock giants by the Almighty based on their ego and pride - every
Montes's stature is in proportion to their pride. Grummel is made of
boulders from the upper mountain and stands only 8 ft. tall. He is the
smallest of the Montes but also the wisest. Having known Emerald's
Father, Sensei Lux, Grummel is drawn to her journey. He helps her win
favor with the Montes clan to give her the "Emerald of Wisdom," but only
under the condition that he accompanies her on her journey. His wisdom
is evident, but his humility and joy make him almost childlike, making
everything lighthearted. Being the only peaceful Montes Giant of the
clan to make a vow against violence, it causes a few hick-ups in their
journey and battles.

**Melea (Light Walker Clan) -** Strong-willed and fierce. The descendant
of one of the most historic warriors of the Light Walker Clan and wife
to Sensei Lux. Melea had to step into a role she never thought was
possible-- Sensei of the Light Walker Clan. After the men were cursed to
the Soulless Citadel, Melea had to step up and become the new leader.
Leading the people into hiding to rebuild in the Forbidden Forrest. She
feels the weight of her leadership while trying to be a mother to
Emerald and Tika. Unlike other women who trained the blade in simple
ways to hunt and keep the tradition, Melea was trained as a young girl
by her father to be one of the fiercest women warriors the Light Walkers
had ever seen. Her father never had a son, so he passed all his training
knowledge to Melea, his firstborn daughter, and taught her the ways of
the blade and the power of the Almighty.

Her Relationship with Emerald is somewhat distant; she expects Emerald
to behave as a daughter of the Sensei should, but Emerald, with her
adventurous heart, can't contain herself.

**Sensei Lux: Light Walker**\
Husband of Melea and Father to Emerald, Lux was the leader of the Light
Walker Clan. A fearless, benevolent, light-hearted ruler and the best
warrior of the Light Walker Clan for centuries. Currently trapped within
the Soulless Citadel, trying to find hope in the darkness of the Citadel
Walls. Lux still rallies his men and keeps the moral up.

![](media/image5.jpg){width="6.5in" height="4.333333333333333in"}

**The Almighty: The Creator**\
The Almighty is. The Almighty has always been. The Almighty will forever
be.​ The Almighty has made all that is - the heavens and the depths, the
elements, the creatures, and the beings - and all are his. Great and
mysterious are his ways. Centuries ago, the Almighty revealed himself to
some of the First Light Walker Clan --- choosing to bestow upon them his
gifts and teaching them his supernatural ways.

The Almighty rarely intervenes in the battle against the dark, but
always guides His faithful---occasionally appearing in human form to
offer wisdom and encouragement.
