**Antonias Story**

Two daughters best friends and sisters born to Sensei Lux the third.
Chaska and Antonia. Antonia the youngest was always yearning for
adventure and loved learning and exploring new things. Chaska was the
oldest mature and responsible. Both believed and thrived in their
Family.

When Antonia was 18 she met a young boy from an other land on her
adventures. He saved her life. They fell in love as she believed and she
took him to her father for permission to marry him. This was out of
custom as the Sensei Lux Family never married out of their own tribe.
Chaska did not trust him but tolerated him out of love for her Sister.
Even Lux embraced him with open arms. He trained him and treated him
like his own son he didn't have. Training him and sharing all their
secrets with him. Everything seemed fine and everyone seemed happy.
Antonia became pregnant with Vegas child.

One night Chaska vilontely woke Antonia up, yelling: Where is the
Ruby?Where is Vegas? I knew he was a bad guy we should have never let
him in and be part of our Family. You always ruin everything with your
careless emotional decision.

Antonia was in shock looking around her and seeing her bed empty. What
was happening. Her heart shattered when she realized the truth. Lux came
running in looking at her with great sadness, behind him his army.
Without saying a word he said to the Man look in the town and forest he
is not here. He left without saying a word.

Chaska slowly leaves the door, saying. This is on you. If you had any
part of it you will suffer the same fate as him. Antonia stayed behind
not being able to think clear, heartbroken she dressed her self and goes
on her own to find her husband. Not knowing Chaska was following her.
While she ventures out she finds clues Vaga left behind for her. On her
walk she is torn hurt by her sister and hurt by the man she loved.
Finally she sees him standing on a boat with a big smile saying to her I
knew you would find me. She rushes to him embracing him than pushing him
away saying what have you done why have you taken the Gem? We have to
take it back now.

Vaga holds her saying: Don't you see what I have done for us? This Gem
will give us and our child everything we need, Power, Success, Fortune..
your father does not know what he holds and refuses to use this mighty
gift. I had to do this for me... for us. Don't you see I am doing this
for us.

Antonia realizes she does not want any part of it and demands him to
give the gem to her, that it is not to late to return. That they will
forgive him..

Vaga looks at her in dismay there is a moment of silent, he looks at her
and says your sister will never accept me and you. As soon as your
father dies she would make sure we are banned...he stretches his hands
out to her.. right when an arrow hits him in his chest and he falls
down. Antonia looks back and sees her sister who shot the arrow. As Vaga
dies in her arms..he tells her he always loved her and that he didn't
want to harm her and only wanted the best. He touches her belly and
takes his last breath. Antonia screams in pain holding the ruby in her
hand on the boat. Chaska is there and tells her to give her the ruby
with no sympathy. Antonia stands up and pushes the boat as it takes sail
she ask her sister. What are you going to do? Kill me like Vaga? Chaska
lowers her bow seeing her sister sail in the sea tears rolling down her
cheak.

Antonia lays in the boat with no food or water in sight she seems to
have taken her last breath soon to. Given up hope and feeling she has
nothing to live for but her child. She feels guilty as she lays there
and starts screaming at the almighty..crying and asking him to save her
child. Than he connects with her telling her: Your son will live and I
promise I will take care of you and him. He will be a wild Man, in
constant struggle with all man but no one will be able to defeat him.
Antonia had peace in her heart and didn't feel alone, knowing that God
has seen her pain.

After her encounter the water started moving under her boat lifting it
up..she was resting on a giant water Monster, seemingly transporting her
through the ocean...when she saw land, the monster gently dove down into
the deep, making sure she was safe on land.

**Life at the new Town**

Antonia hides the gem and starts a quiet life in the town of the island

One night the Gem started glowing and shining, she tried to hide it in a
vase but the gem broke free shining a light up to the night sky.
Terrified she grabbed Benaijah and the gem and started running out of
town. In that moment as she looks back she sees a boat heading for the
island she knew that they came for... the first boat lands and the
pirates mixed with a hoard of forgotten ones start killing everyone and
everything in their way. In the far distance she saw an other ship
appear and taking course to Tortuga Island. Antonia runs up the hill
trying to escape and hide. She hides Ben in a tree and tells him not to
come out unless its her. Right after that she is surrounded by forgotten
ones, just like the ones she knew from her home town. She tries to fight
them summoning the strength of the almighty. She is strong and knows how

her up on her neck asking for the gem, as he chokes her a fight breaks
out she can't see much as she passes out calling for her son.. the last
thing she sees is a pirate standing over her.

Dave learned the ways of his father and became know as Captain Davy.

Blackbeard highest goal was to return to the Realm of Man, also called
