It was way past midnight, and Emerald was still lying in bed with
wide-open eyes. It was her 14th birthday the next week, and she was
still unsure if she was ready for it. "A Light Walker Woman Warrior."
she thought. It had a nice ring to it, but becoming a Woman Warrior also
came with a lot of responsibility. And well, a mature woman fights for
her clan. She kills Forgotten Ones, protects the village, and most
importantly, seeks the staff as a Hogo-Sha.

Emerald felt that if she could somehow prove to herself that she was
worthy of doing this, of fighting and protecting, then maybe she would
feel comfortable stepping into womanhood. I mean, a little self-proving
adventure never hurt anybody, right?

Question: Adventure -- yes or no?

Right answer: Yes

Emerald's memories of her father, Sensei Lux, are still as clear as a
spring sunrise. No matter how long ago it seemed, Emerald could always
see his gentle but firm, loving face. His eyes always seemed to be full
of life and wisdom. "But such a goof," she smiled and thought. So many
incredible memories and she wished she had more with her father, but she
was at least happy to keep his memories alive. The bedtime stories, in
particular, were her favorite. Sensei Lux and his adventures with the
Rock Giants, the Lacus water people, the staff and its origin, and
always more about the Almighty, but she had a favorite. She always
returned to her favorite--the one he always used to tell whenever she
seemed a bit upset. "Sensei Lux steals a Dragon Egg." He always told
it so funny and lifelike. She could remember rolling in her bed laughing
at his extravagant facial expressions. She lay in bed smiling. "Wait a
minute. I can do this," Emerald thought to herself. "I, Daughter to
Sensei Lux, the next keeper of the Dragon Egg! This! This could be it!"
Emerald started working out a plan in her head already. What do you
think?

"Okay, I'm out of the village... but where to head to now?"

"Now that sounds like a great idea... Go to the Canyon of Despair...

The Canyon of Despair is never far off, as it reaches through half of

Question: From below or from above?

Dropping from above seemed to have been the right call. The path looked
