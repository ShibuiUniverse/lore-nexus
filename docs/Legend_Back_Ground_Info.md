Chapter 2: Women Warriors**

1.  **Melea** - Wife of Sensei lux, and new Light Walker Clan leader

2.  **Emerald** - Daughter of Melea and Sensei Lux, Protagonist of our
    > 1st screenplay. Good side of the Prophecy of The Blade of Eternity

3.  **The Wise One** - "Nana" - Sensei Lux's Mother and known as the
    > wise prophet in the clan and head of the "Wise Counsel" - Named
    > Emerald based off a dream from The Almighty.

4.  **Antonia** - Melea's Aunt and keeper of the Baransu Ruby (possibly
    > Melea's Sister now)

5.  **Kuso** - Light Walker turned to Dark one by the Dark Whisper.
    > After her father was taken to the Soulless Citadel, her mother
    > died of illness. The Whisper got into her head - planting lies
    > about the Almighty - How could he let this happen to you? If he
    > loved you, he wouldn't take your father and your mother away in
    > the same year. She is the antagonist of our first screenplay. She
    > is the second person in the Prophecy of the Blade to Eternity. She
    > opens up the shadow realm with the Blade.

6.  **Dark One** - A true dark one that has been consumed by the
    > darkness and pledged her soul to Khanonai - No backstory here - it
    > was purely to show what a Women Warrior of the Dark ones fully
    > consumed could look like. Similar to the Forgotten ones above,
    > except these Dark Ones have CHOSEN to follow khaonai and his
    > teachings. The current dark ones are very cultish.

7.  **Dark Tracker** Known as "Tracker" - Along with her beast, she can
    > track any Women Warrior seeking the Staff of the Almighty. She is
    > Kuso's right-wing - Relentlessly pursuing Emerald on the first
    > Screenplay.

8.  **Fortifina** - a legendary warrior and Hogo-Sha, she's not one you
    > want to meet in the battlefield. She has a seat at the "Wise
    > Counsel" - Not sure what her backstory is fully, but she has an
    > appearance and fight scene in the screenplay

9.  **Akina** - Some say she is socially awkward, but she just prefers
    > her scaled friends over the others in the clan. Not really sure
    > her background, but I like to call her the Dragon Trainer. At some
    > point, I am thinking of bringing her in with the dragons she has
    > trained.

10. **Missy Hunter** - The Best in the land with a Bow. Known for her
    > leadership and training of upcoming Women Warriors and her ability
    > to clip the wings off a fly with a single shot. She a leader of
    > the "Wise Counsel"\
