JAMES'S VOICE CHECKLIST:

Before posting, ask:

☑ Would I actually say this out loud?

☑ Is this ME or am I performing "fantasy author"?

☑ Did I use at least one contraction?

☑ Is there a moment of levity even in darkness?

☑ Am I being transparent/honest/real?

☑ Does this sound like a human, not a brand?

RED FLAGS (rewrite if you see these):

🚩 "Join us on this epic journey"

🚩 "Greetings, fellow adventurers"

🚩 Three+ sentences without personality

🚩 Explaining emotions instead of showing

🚩 Apologizing for being indie

🚩 Trying to sound "professional"

EMERGENCY VOICE RESET:

If a post feels off, rewrite it like you're texting a friend about
Shibui. That's the tone.
