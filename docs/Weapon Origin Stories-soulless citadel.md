They are traditionally made from bone or shell to symbolize the ancestors that came before them.  It is seen as a way for the first born to take their family with them to wherever the journey ahead takes them.  Legend says that the love infused into them during their creation has magical protective properties that will aid their wearer if harm comes their way.*  
*\~The Lightwalkers- A Clan History*

***Hawaiian Shirt***

*“I was there with my family, on vacation\!  We had decided to visit the tropics and found a cozy hotel on the beach; my mother always loved the beach.  It was supposed to be in celebration of my eighteenth birthday and I was so excited.  I was relaxing there on the beach and sharing a drink with my father, as my sister and mother played in the water a little ways away.  My father looked at the time, and sighed.  He then told me he loved me and was so proud of me.  He said not to be scared.  I was confused, but before I could question him about what I shouldn't be scared of, it happened.  All light faded away, like I was blacking out.  Now I am here\!  Where am I?  What is this place?  WHO ARE YOU?”*

*This is the firsthand account of the newest arrival.  I feel sorry for the boy, he had no clue what had just happened to him.*

*\~Taken from the journal of Master Vi inside the Citadel*
