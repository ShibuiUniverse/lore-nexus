# **THE MARK OF A TRAITOR**

**Kage's Fall to Darkness \- Full Backstory**

**Written by James Fazio \- Eternal Blade \- © Shibui Labs, LLC**

She was eight years old when they took her father. Like every other Light Walker child, Kage watched as Tenebris' curse ripped the men from their lives and banished them into the Soulless Citadel. The entire clan mourned, but they found ways to keep going despite the unbearable loss. But for Kage, the nightmare was just beginning.

After the clan had found their new home in the Forbidden Forest, life was moving on for most, but for some, the loss was too much. Two years later, her mother died. Not from battle. Not from a Dark One's blade. From a common cold and a broken heart. She was just ten years old, both parents gone, and more alone than she had ever felt before. The feeling that there is no one left in the world who knows at all who you are. The other families tried to help. They brought food and offered to have her join them at their homestead, but she declined. A void was growing that could possibly never be filled. Nothing could bring back what she'd lost.

And that's when she heard it. *The Whisper.*

It started small, just thoughts that felt like her own. *Why did this happen to you? If the Almighty loved you, would He have taken everything? He abandoned you. Look at you, all alone. It’s HIS fault\!* At first, she resisted... She remembered her father's teachings. She tried to connect with the Almighty as her mother taught her, but the Whisper was patient. It knew grief. It knew rage. It knew exactly when to speak and when to let silence do the work.

For four years, the Whisper cultivated her bitterness like a garden. A deadly garden that would poison many souls for years to come. By fourteen, Kage had stopped seeking the Almighty altogether. By sixteen, she had stopped believing. Numbness, as if life itself wasn’t even a reality anymore, but someone else was walking in her shoes. By eighteen, she had pledged herself to Khaonai and the Dark Whisper. A choice that would echo for generations to come.

There was a woman named Akari who tried to save her. She was one of the few who had actually tried to *reach* Kage after her mother died. Not just with food or empty comfort—Akari spent time with her. She trained with her, tried to discuss things of the spirit, and just listened when Kage finally broke down her walls of bitterness, but Akari saw the darkness creeping in. She saw the corruption before anyone noticed, and she fought to pull her back toward the light. "You are not alone, Kage. Many things pass, some unfair. Some feel outright wrong. I know your pain, but you can’t let it consume you." But Kage couldn't hear it anymore. The Whisper had rooted too deep.

When Kage finally left the Forbidden Forest to join the Dark Ones, Akari was the one who begged the Wise Counsel to give her more time. To not give up on the girl. To believe she could still be saved. "She's just eighteen years old," Akari had said. "Her entire life has been pain and loss. We can’t just abandon her the way she thinks the Almighty did." The Counsel agreed. They would wait, they would hope, but they had no idea what was coming.

Time passed. Kage quite enjoyed the darkness. *Why fight how you feel?* *Let your anger guide you. Let it fuel you\!* The Whisper had been testing her loyalty with small tasks, minor acts of cruelty, each one pushing her further from the light. Each one leading her down a path of no return, but the Whisper saw the potential in Kage. The darkness that lingered where no one else saw. Khaonai demanded the Whisper to test her. *"If your allegiance trully restwith us,"* the Whisper hissed, *"prove it. Bring me into the Forbidden Forest. Open the gates. Let my servants feast on the Light Walkers who abandoned you."*

Kage knew the forest's defenses. She knew how the Light Walkers navigated the constantly shifting and deadly forest, and she knew their patrol routes. She knew exactly how to get the Dark Ones inside. And she did it. Midnight, the gates opened from within. Kage led a small horde of Dark beasts and Dark Trackers into the heart of the Forbidden Forest—the place she once called home.

But the Light Walkers weren't as unprepared as the Dark Ones thought. Melea had suspected something. Call it intuition. Call it the Almighty's guidance. Call it her motherly instincts, but she had doubled the patrols and set extra guards. When Kage opened the gates, the Light Walkers were ready.

The battle was swift and brutal. The Light Walkers' blade never missed. All their training, all the preparations, the Light Walkers cut through the Forgotten Ones like they were nothing. Arrows found their marks, blades sang through corrupted flesh. Within minutes, it was clear—this attack was over before it began. Kage stood in the center of the carnage, surrounded by the bodies of the creatures she'd led to slaughter.

That's when Akari stepped forward. Not with a blade drawn, not with anger in her eyes, but with tears.

"Kage," she said, her voice breaking. "It’s not too late. It’s never too late. The Almighty has not abandoned you. Just come back to us. Come home*.*"

Kage felt something inside her chest crack. Something small. Something that hadn't completely died yet. The girl who used to seek the Almighty’s ways. The girl who used to believe in something bigger, but the Whisper was louder. *"Kill her. Prove you are mine. Prove you are who I think you are... the greatest of my creations."*

*The greatest of my creations?* She thought*.* Kage's hand went to her blade.

"Kage, please—" Akari stepped closer, unarmed, hands open. "I know you're hurting. I know you're angry. But you don't have to do this. You don't have to—"

A sound that Kage would never forget, her blade running through the chest of the only person left who loved her. Akari's eyes went wide. Not with fear. With heartbreak. "I... I forgive you," she whispered, blood on her lips. Then she fell.

“AKARI\! NO\!” Melea shouted as she charged forward.

The Light Walkers attacked. Kage fought back, but she was outnumbered. Within seconds, they had her disarmed and on her knees. Melea knelt beside Akari's body and placed her head in her arms. The woman was still breathing...barely. Still trying to speak. Still trying to reach Kage even in her final moments. "Come back... to the light..." Akari's hand reached weakly toward Kage. "It's... not too late..." Then she was gone.

Melea closed Akari's eyes and held her for a long moment as her tears dripped onto Akari’s lifeless body.  Then stood and walked toward Kage with her katana drawn. Kage expected execution. Welcomed it, even. Better to die than to live with what she'd just done. But Melea didn't kill her.

Melea looked Kage in the eyes. Saw the flicker of something still human behind the darkness. Something she couldn’t just cut down. *Akari wouldn’t have wanted it*. "You were one of us," Melea said quietly. "You were *family.*" Then she pressed the edge of her blade to Kage's face. One swift, deliberate cut from the top of her left eyebrow down to her cheek.

Kage screamed. Blood poured down her face, but Melea didn't flinch.

"I will not murder you in cold blood," Melea said, her voice like iron. "That is what separates us from *you.* You will walk Baransu with the mark of a traitor. What you've done here today... you are a Light Walker no more."

Kage screamed. Blood and tears mixed and dripped off her face. Melea stood, sheathed her blade, and turned her back on Kage. "If I ever see you again," Melea said without looking back, "it will be as enemies."

Kage stumbled out of the Forbidden Forest alone. Her face was ruined. Her heart was... something worse than ruined. The Whisper was ecstatic. *"Good. You are stronger than I thought. You’ve become more. Something they can not hurt, and now we will rule together..."*

Kage touched the wound on her face. It would scar. Badly. She would wear this mark for the rest of her life. The mark of a traitor. And in that moment, something inside her... settled. A flame ignited in her. A fire to never be extinguished. Fine. If they wanted a traitor? She'd give them a traitor. If they wanted a monster? She'd become the monster. If they wanted the darkness? She'd bring them darkness they couldn't imagine.

She rose to her feet, blood still dripping down her face, and walked deeper into the shadows. By the time the wound healed, the old Kage was gone. And in her place stood the leader of the Dark Ones. The huntress. The one who would chase Emerald to the ends of Baransu. The woman who would stop at nothing to claim the Eternal Blade and open the Shadow Realm. Not because the Whisper commanded it. But because she had nothing left to lose.

She was Khaonai’s and the Light Walkers would pay.
