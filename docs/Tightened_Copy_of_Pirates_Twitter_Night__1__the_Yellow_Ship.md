**Caribbean Sea near Crooked Island - 1718**

Fresh from their latest raid, the notorious Pirate fleet of Captain Vane
let down their guard at Crooked Island. Rum free-flowed throughout the
fleet - a quite familiar stench filled this air -- sea salt, dried
blood, and rum. Vane checked his reflection in his sword and wiped the
blood of an unlucky Spaniard from his brow. The sword was no ordinary
blade. Given to Vane as a gift by the witch of Cat Island - rumored to
have been crafted by dark magic to make curses and to break them. Vane's
men believed the witch put a spell on the sword and kept their distance
from it. But Vane sensed that its power was far older - and far more
dangerous.

"Navy Ships to the fleet stern!" the Phoenix's lookout shouted.\
"Damnit! Ye best sober up quickly, lads! Singal to the rest of the
fleet!" Vane ordered. The men jolted into action. Vane knew they were
outgunned by the Navy warships, but the pirate sloops were swifter than
the Navy's galleys.

But McCray's "Plover" was bringing up the rear of the pirate fleet, and
the Navy was closing in on her. The youngest of the fleet's captains,
McCray had taken to the sea for His Majesty in the Spanish War as a mere
lad. What he lacked in years, he made up for in courage and a short
fuse. Only his First Mate Matthews could talk sense into him when he got
his blood up. But what they saw drained the rum right out of them -
three Navy ships were coming up on them fast. "They were waiting for us
to round the passage!"

**McCray's Question:** Do you make a run for it as Vane commanded?
OR...do you make ready your cannon and signal back to Vane to fight?

**Answer 1:[Make a run for it.]{.underline}**

"Hoist the sails, boys; it's time for this bird to fly!" shouted McCray,
and Matthews gave the commands. In a flash, the sails unfurled, and the
Plover lurched forward at impressive speed. But it wasn't fast enough.
Captain Julius ordered the Navy ships to corner the closest of the
pirate fleet in hopes of drawing Vane out.

**Answer 2: [Make ready your cannon and convince Vane to
fight.]{.underline}**

"Make ready the cannon! And signal back to the Phoenix we have to
fight!" shouted McCray. Matthews commanded the crew to prepare, and the
signal was sent.

Immediately, the lookout spied the Phoenix's return message, "Capt'n,
the Phoenix signals again to run!"

"Damn...damn!"

"C'mon McCray, I seen ye do it before..." said Vane under his breath,
then louder with a glint in his dark eyes - "Come get me ya Lobsters -
it's me ya want, not them small pickin's."

Cyrus implored Vane, "Captain - McCray's surrounded..."

"Can't turn back - that's what they want us to do. Full speed ahead!
(then under his breath) C'mon McCray, don't go yellow on me."

McCray watched as the pirate fleet distanced themselves from the Navy -
and from the Plover.

"Vane!!" The Plover was trapped and outnumbered. McCray knew his wits
were no match for this cluster. He wiped his brow and took a deep
breath...

**McCray's Question:** Do you -\
Go down fighting and take down as many of those damn Bluejackets as
possible?\
OR raise the white flag to surrender and save your necks?

**Answer** 1: [Go down fighting]{.underline}

"Ahoy, ya yellybellied scabs!" McCay yelled to his crew, "Today's the
day we finally show these Navy boys how to fight! Prepare the cannons!"

The crew burst into action with some quick aye-ayes and got off a shot
that split the Navy flagship's yardarm.

"Arrrr! Give 'em hell, mateys! Back 'em off so we can get some elbow
room", shouted Matthews.

But the Navy's Captain Julius ordered return fire from each Navy ship,
and the Plover took hits from all sides.

The Plover's cannons and gunners were blown to kingdom come, and
deckhands took fire from a cannon that struck broadsides. When the
Plover's main sail was hit, McCray knew it was all over. They were
boarded and captured - McCray with a pistol to his temple and his men at
gunpoint of His Majesty's marines.

**Answer 2: [Surrender ]{.underline}**

"Well, boys," announced McCray, "There ain't no way out of this one.
Hoist the white flag - them Lobsters will take us to trial in Kingston.
So, look sharp. We'll live to fight another day!"

The crew hoisted the sign for surrender, and the Navy sent a boarding
party to the Plover. As the whistle blew to announce the Navy Captain's
arrival, Matthews asked, "What's the plan, Cap'n?".

McCray returned, "Vane'll come to Kingston - we need to buy time."

McCray and his crew were brought in irons before the Magistrate of
Kingston. "Remember the code, boys..." said McCray to his men before the
Magistrate in a traditional wig slammed down his gavel.

"His Majesty's Court finds these men before us guilty of piracy. Captain
McCray, as a man of service, you swore an oath. You and this rabble are
a disgrace!

You are hereby sentenced to imprisonment for the remainder of your lives
in Kingston jail. Or...if you accept His Majesty's mercy and reform - by
capturing and returning your former master Charles Vane - you will be
granted pardon."

**McCray's Question:** Do you keep your honor and go to jail? OR do you
take the pardon and betray the pirate code and Vane?

**Answer 1:** [Keep your honor, keep to the code, and go to
jail.]{.underline}

"Well, your Honor, you see, I've mended my faithless ways, and I just
can't betray my Commander. Looks like we'll be new residents of your
fine jail here in Kingston."

Infuriated, the Magistrate slammed his gavel down. "Take them out of my
sight. Now!"

The guards came to collect McCray and his crew to haul them out of the
court. But instead of taking them to the jail, they were taken to the
courtyard where a dozen nooses were set.

"What's this?!" demanded McCray.

"Wrong choice, pirate," said the head guard.\
"Woah, jus a second ye scurvy. Ye want Captain Vain or not?" McCray

**Answer 2:** [Take the pardon, regain your ship, and agree to capture
Vane]{.underline}

McCray stood and addressed the Magistrate, "Your Honor, you've shown us
the error of our ways. I thank you kindly for your generous pardon and
the return of my ship - and to find Commander... I mean - the Pirate
Vane - for His Majesty's Navy. We'll need a month's time for ship
repairs, then will find and return the villain here for justice."

At these words, McCray's crew began to whisper to each other in
disbelief - what was McCray doing? But Matthews gave them a look to say,
"Trust your Captain," and they quieted down.

The Magistrate smirks as they walk back through the doors, "Wise choice,
Mr. McCray. You must return with Vane - alive or dead - by Autumn's
cool, or your men here will swing on All Hallow's Eve. You'd better make
those repairs a lot faster."

The Magistrate ordered Matthews and 7 others of McCray's men to be
hauled away by the jailers. McCray's face fell. "Between the devil and
the deep sea..." he muttered as he exchanged a look with Matthews.

Now pirate hunters, McCray and his crew set sail to find Vane.

"What are ye gonna do when we find him, Cap'n?" said Old Pete, McCray's
longtime lookout, as they made their way out of Kingston Harbor.

McCray didn't respond - a look of worry settled on his brow.

Some days later, nearing the Cays, the Plover came upon some gentle
inlets with outcroppings of rock that could hide them.

McCray ordered, "Tuck in and drop anchor here for the night - we'll make
our way to the Cays in the morn."

That night, McCray dreamed fitfully of a ghostly yellow ship sailing
towards him - it would not slow or change course. It got so close he
could see a withered shade of a captain on deck, clutching his chest and
telling him to turn around. McCray woke in a sweat just before the ship
reached him.

**Question:** Do you return to Kingston and try to sneak ashore to
spring your men? That dream seemed like a clear warning! OR...Do you
press on - you're not the superstitious type, and you know that you'll
be caught and hung if you return to Kingston without Vane.

**Answer 1:** [Sail back to Kingston - you'll have to spring the boys
yourself.]{.underline}

As soon as the sun broke over the horizon, McCray was at the helm and
bellowed, "Set course for Kingston!" His crew was astounded - go back to
certain death?

"Cap'n?", said Old Pete.

"An ill wind blows to the Cays...and to Vane. We have to go back for the
boys ourselves." The crew scurried to positions, and the Plover rounded
the last of the inlets to set sail for Kingston when the lookout spotted
the Phoenix, Vane's flagship - alone without the fleet. The Phoenix had
dropped anchor - almost like it was waiting....

**Answer 2:** [Press on to find Vane - your boys' lives depend on
it.]{.underline}

At the first light of morning, McCray gave the order to make for the
Cays - the longtime secret hideout of the pirate fleet. But just as they
rounded the last of the inlets to set sail on the open sea, what did
they see but the Phoenix, Vane's flagship - alone without the fleet. The
Phoenix had dropped anchor - almost like it was waiting....

**Question:** Do you reassure Vane of your loyalty and convince him to
help free your men from Kingston? OR... Your ship has more cannon than
Vane's Phoenix and no other ships to back him up. Do you capture Vane
and take him to Kingston in order to save your men?

**Answer 1/ Ending 1:** [Reassure Vane of your loyalty and urge him to
help you free your crew.]{.underline}

"Hoist the Roger with Vane's colors - show him we've been true!" shouted
McCray. But no sooner had they hoisted it than the Phoenix sailed behind
an outcropping and was hidden from sight. It was then McCray felt that
familiar cold metal of a pistol's barrel against his temple. It was
Vane - he'd boarded the Plover in secret to discover McCray's
intentions.

"Thought you could bring me in, did ye, whelp? Why, you're nothing but a
coward", seethed Vane.

"No, Commander!, implored McCray. We've been true! Followed the code!
Magistrate's got eight of me crew in Kingston jail - we need the fleet's
help to spring 'em!"

"Likely story - that why the Navy's following you?" said Vane. "You led
'em right to me."

McCray's face turned white - "Wha-?"

Sure enough, turning the corner of the last inlet appeared the Union
Jack on three Navy ships.

Vane instructed McCray to hold position until he could escape. "Then
you'll set a course to lead the Lobsters off my tail."

"But Commander, I swear - I had no idea they were tailing us!" pleaded
McCray.

"You've betrayed your brethren, McCray - and now you'll pay."

As Vane uttered his sentence, he unsheathed his sword with his other
hand and drew blood in the shape of an "X" over McCray's heart. "This
wound will never heal - it will fester. It will never kill you but will
never let you die." And now he boomed to the crew, "Your yellow-hearted
captain will turn this ship, and you mates to wisps, shadows of
yourselves - cursed to sail the sea forever with ne'er a shore to call
home."

And with that, Vane sheathed his sword and launched himself over the
deck and into his landing boat. McCray collapsed, and his men rushed to
him. Some tried to abandon ship, but the curse held them forcibly to the
deck, their skin already yellowing.

And now the yellowed Plover is a sailing graveyard for McCray and his
crew, cursed as cowards to roam the seas - forever hunting for Vane and
the pirate fleet. But they can never fully die, nor can they rest 'til
Vane releases them or until they hunt him down and deliver him to
Kingston.
