**Game Lore for WAR FOR ZILLARNIA**

STORY PURPOSE: To give lore and an understanding of why the Light
Walkers are fighting within the Citadel and create something fun for the
Game.

**Journal Entry for Sensei Lux:**

*Every day is the same. No sunrise. No sunset. There are no more days.
No way to count the time passing by. For all I know, we have been here
for years.*

*I haven't heard the voice of The Almighty since that day. Not even a
sense of the almighty power surging through my veins. A connection I
long for, now stripped from my grasp along with everything I love.
[Stumbling around in the darkness of my mind. Where is the light
gone?]{.mark}*

*And my family? What has become of them? My hope has withered. What went
wrong that fateful day? Does it even matter? It is hard to find hope in
salvation while the only real thing in front of me is despair and
darkness...the Citadel's damp, fortifying walls and the ongoing mental
struggle to hold onto anything left of what we once were. No one ages a
day here. How could a soul, without a soul, age anyway? How is a leader
supposed to lead in a place like this?*

**IN THE CITADEL**

New generations of firstborns arrived in the Citadel here and there. The
"Innocent," as they came to be known by the others, were cursed into
this purgatory. Some dressed in clothes that seemed odd to the original
warriors. How much time had gone by since that awful day?

"Son," an older Mythic warrior asked a new Innocent that arrived,
"May I ask you a question? How fond are you of that red outerwear
garment you so confidently flaunt?"

"Who, me?" the young Innocent responded.

"No, no. The other one wearing the bright red jacket with diamonds
shining brighter than the sun. Yes, you."

"It's the King's Jacket. I'm quite fond of it, and acquiring one
took me a great deal."

"Well, you see, son, I've been wearing this godforsaken Yoroi for, I
am not sure how long at this point. The thing we like to do around here
to pass the time is swap our clothes around. You get some cursed
warrior's gear, and we get to try out your new gadgets. I quite like
those eye blinder things you've got as well. So shall we make a trade?
My Yoroi for your "King's Jacket." I'll throw in this Octopus too.
Just don't eat him. We've become quite close."

"Unk, you are weirding me out right now. So, no, I would not like to
trade, and no, I don't want your weird Octopus pet or whatever you got
going on there."

"Alright, alright, son... Give it some time. That Jacket is mine one
way or another," The old mythic smirks, "If you won't trade it, I
guess I'll just have to win it in battle at the Arena."

The longer they stayed in the Citadel, the more their appearance began
to change. Eyes lost their light, their skin unnatural - bitterness
crept into the hearts of the Soulless in the Citadel. There was a
presence in the Citadel. The Darkness still loomed. At your weakest
moment, a voice would come to you. It was not a visible being, but a
glimpse of a shadow or an ever-moving fog sometimes accompanied it. A
voice that resembled the utterings of Tenebris, but this one was
different. Somehow this voice did not come off as a Dark Warlord hungry
for power but as a suave and deceptive voice. It commanded great
confidence. When this voice came to you, somehow it knew everything
about you- and not just what you'd done, but your thoughts,
insecurities, and desires.

"Young man," an eerie whisper fluttered into an innocent's ear. "Do
please tell me, if the Almighty were to care so much for you, why would
he not just open up the Gates of this Citadel? He is the all powerful
creator after all isn't he? Surely he could just easily free you. But,
that is if he even cares."

"Who... Who is there?" the innocent responded, "I know not what you
speak of."

"Oh, my boy, but you will... In time. You will know the hopelessness
you shall endure here. I've got time, all the time in the world truly.
But... I can promise you freedom, or maybe you'd like to return and see
your loved ones? Maybe, Talia? She would be so delighted to see your
face again. I am sure." hissed the dark whisper.

"Wait, Talia? How do you know of Talia? Not even my mother knew of
her?"

"Oh, yes, yes. We know of Talia. She is quite beautiful. It'd be a
shame for her to marry another in your absence. Now, wouldn't it?"

"What?! Tell me more about Talia? I want to know how she is doing."

"Oh, my son, but of course. I can give you so much more than just
information."

Many of the brave and hardened warriors from the Light Walker Clan had
fallen to this voice's manipulative, sharp, and deceiving words. The
promises, the lies, and the persistence of this voice turned many
against each other. Division and discord plagued the Citadel and the
warriors within. Sensei Lux had to act before his men were torn apart
from within.

But there was another voice as well -

"Young brother", Lux called to an Innocent who had recently arrived and
kept his own company, "What is that sweet song you are humming?"

Startled from his thoughts, the Innocent bowed with respect to Lux as he
wiped a betraying tear-

"It is the lullaby of our mothers and the cry of our loves since the
curse. I sing it so I do not forget..."

"Nitaga, moku diy Niryolu-na m'beraga ---

Yol Ng'ofuror s'teraga.

Nitaga, moku diy Aisnay-umeni m'beraga ---

Osem Ng'ofuror s'teraga.

M'pashor, Ng'cabor, Ng'okor, S'kir!

Ng'yodoraga knai, M'odoraga knai.

Belshul, Katem, Yostem, Musulay

Sku Funa, Eia, Tanutay

Ng'yeremir M'oala"

"We, who were many days in the sunlight ---

We have forgotten the sun.

We, who wept many days for our loves ---

We have forgotten fear.

Plunge, Bellow, Fend, Carve!

We cannot fall, We cannot return.

All warriors, walls, fortresses, enemies

will surrender, like flowers,

and smiles and song."

Lux was taken aback -

He called a new order - The Arena. A place to fight the cunning whisper
and its deceptive lies. A place to continually hone one's skills for
the coming battle for Freedom... or so they hoped.

"There is no death in this place! We, Light Walkers, have never feared
the taste of death. We've all battled our inner thoughts, planted by the
whispers of Khaonai, but we won't be overcome. Every day we fight!
Every day we clear our minds. We focus on our enemy. We focus on what's
at stake. The fight is not against each other. Our fight is to hold onto
hope of what can still be!"

Gesturing to the Innocent who sang the lullaby, he continued, "As of
now, we have a new song -

"Nitaga, moku diy Niryolu-na m'beraga ---

Yol harine utonkibiraga.

Nitaga, moku diy Aisnay-umen m'beraga ---

Lamatay harine s'keraga.

M'pashor, Ng'cabor, Ng'okor, S'kir!

Ng'yodor m'oaraga knai, M'odor m'oaraga.

Shu Belshul, Bostem, Musulay ng'yeremiraga knai!

Shanas Zilarnumen risainaga da tanutaga m'berila"

"We, who were many days in the sunlight ---

Today we carry the Sun.

We, who wept many days for our loves ---

[Today we dry our tears]{.mark}.

Plunge, Bellow, Fend, Carve!

We will not fall, We will return.

We surrender to no warrior, no fortress nor enemy,

The Hope of Zillarnia is our shield, our song"

Sensei Lux shouted, "Once you step into that arena, you plant firm your
mind. Not for me, not for yourself, but for your people. For your
family. We fight for the Light!"
