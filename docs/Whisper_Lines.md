# Whisper Lines

Two voices haunt the Lorekeeper — the dark whisperer (crimson) and the Almighty (warm ivory). Both fade in at edges of the screen, drift upward, and escalate through 4 stages as the user lingers. Light fires at half the cadence of dark.

Edit the lines below and I'll sync them into `src/components/ui/DarkWhisper.tsx`.

---

## DARK WHISPERS (crimson, the whisperer)

### Stage 1 — `0:08+` — opens the door
- I see you.
- I know you.
- Still here?
- I still live.
- Give in.
- Give up.

### Stage 2 — `2:00+` — leans in
- What has He done for you?
- Why fight how you feel?
- Stay a little longer.
- He abandoned you.
- I've got time. All the time in the world.
- We've been watching.

### Stage 3 — `5:00+` — gets in your head
- You can't win.
- The Almighty doesn't answer everyone.
- I can give you power.
- Let your anger guide you.
- I can promise you power.
- The Almighty is weak... like you.

### Stage 4 — `10:00+` — drops the mask
- Soulless you shall become.
- I shall return.
- Prove yourself to me.
- I have been waiting.
- The darkness always was.
- You are weak. Weak!
- True power comes from me.

## LIGHT WHISPERS (warm ivory, the Almighty)

### Stage 1 — `0:08+` — presence
- You are seen.
- Be still.
- Breathe.
- Walk on.
- Take heart.
- Look up.

### Stage 2 — `2:00+` — answers the doubt
- I have not left.
- You were never alone.
- I am yours and you are mine.
- Lift your eyes.
- Listen — closer.
- Let the light guide you.

### Stage 3 — `5:00+` — wisdom + presence
- Be still and know.
- Mercy walks with you.
- You are loved.
- You are held.
- Your light is yours alone.
- Trust the way before you.

### Stage 4 — `10:00+` — declarations
- I am with you.
- Walk where I lead.
- You were named in love.
- The morning belongs to you.
- Rise up.
- I will be your shield.
- The story is not over.
